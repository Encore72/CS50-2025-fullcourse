1) The audio aura could be characterized with something resembling the following query:
        SELECT AVG(energy), AVG (valence), AVG (danceability)
        FROM songs
        WHERE user_id = (SELECT id FROM users WHERE name = ?)
        GROUP BY audio_aura
        LIMIT 100

   The ? is a placeholder value that should be replaced by the id or name of the user
   requesting his wrapped.


2) If we understand Audio Aura as moods, and we understand mood to be calculated only with avg energy,
   valence and danceability, then we should not look at top 100 most listened songs for several reasons,
   for example:

        a) User might have an extremely bad(or good) month throughout the year where he uses Spotify a lot
        or maybe he doesn't use it at all. Either way the mood of that month will be overrepresented
        or not represented at all.

        b) User might use the Spotify app during exercising, and maybe during exercising he always
        listens to the same genre of music, no matter his mood. This will cause missrepresentation.

        c) An all time favorite artist for the user might release an album in that year, that would mean that
        music would be overplayed and overrepresented, no matter the user's mood.

        d) User might play inspiring music to get motivated to endure a task, but in reality he is depressed
        or feeling down, he listens to uplifting music to counter his true "aura".
