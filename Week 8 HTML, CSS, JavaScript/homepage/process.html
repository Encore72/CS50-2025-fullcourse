TL:DR How to make good Espresso
Budget Friendly Edition

Step 3: The Process

  1. 1. Weigh the coffee grain.
    • Use a digital scale to measure your coffe dose precisely.
    • Single dose: aim for 8gr. Double dose: aim for 16gr.
    • Consistency is key for repeatable results.
  2. 2. Grinding the coffee. (Unless you are using pre-grinded coffee and a pressurized basket).
    • You should grind right before brewing, for the freshest coffee and best results.
    • Adjusting how fine or coarse your grinding is, will be the most difficult task.
    • The golden rule is 1:2 brew ratio in 25-30s. (e.g., for 8gr of coffee you should extract 16g or ml of espresso in 25-30 seconds of brewing).
    • If after 25-30s of brewing your yield is higher, your grind is too coarse; if your yield is lower, your grind is too fine.
  3. 3. Distribute the grinded coffee.
    • Fill your basket with the freshly grinded coffee.
    • Gently tap the portafilter to evenly level the grinded coffee in the basket.
    • Optional: Use your WDT tool to break up clumps.
  4. 4. Tamp
    • Press down firmly and evenly with the tamper, apply about 30 pounds of pressure.
    • Make sure the puck is properly leveled to avoid wrong channeling.
  5. 5. Brew
    • Lock the portafilter into the group head of your coffee machine.
    • Optional: for best results, warm up your machine before your shot by making it run some water into an empty cup.
    • Start the shot, time it (25-30s) and weigh it (1:2 brew ratio).
    • Learn to diagnose your shot: Look for a steady, thick golden stream of espresso.
    • If you didn't get it, go back to step 2 and adjust your grind.
  6. 6. Enjoy!
    • Remember to clean the portafilter and group head after use.
