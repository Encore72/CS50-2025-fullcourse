TL:DR How to make good Espresso
Budget Friendly Edition

Step 1: The Equipment

Basic

  • Espresso Machine The best starter machine is the "De'longhi ECP 3420" in USA, which is called "De'longhi ECP 33.21" in Europe. You can find it for around 100-120$. Honorable mention as well to the "De'longhi Dedica" which you can also find for around 100-140$. If you want to spend more but in budget, the "Breville Bambino" will be your best option.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

  • Grinder Unless you want to roll with the presurized basket that comes with your espresso machine and pre-grounded coffee beans (it will already yield better results than your typical Nespresso pods), for the best results you will need a Grinder. In all fairness, the grinder is the most important element of your setup. Withouth a good grinder you won't be able to grind the beans fine enough, and you won't get good shots. The best price-quality grinder is the KINGRINDER K4 or KINGRINDER K6. You can find them for around 50-100$. Also honorable mention to the Baratza ESP (120$).
    If you decide to get a grinder, you must at least get the 2 following advanced items.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

Advanced

  • Bottomless portafilter and Non-pressurized basket To better diagnose your shots, you will need a bottomless portafilter, this will allow you to directly see how your brew is pouring. To complement your bottomless portafilter you will need a non-pressurized basket, this will allow you to get the best out of your machine. Two non-pressurized baskets are recommended (single dose and double dose).

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

  • Tamper A proper tamper to make sure your puck is compacted correctly and not cock-eyed is recommended. Better than the low quality plastic tamper that comes with your machine.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

  • Precision Scale Consistency is key. The best way to obtain consistency is to always grind the same dose of coffee. You will not be able to consistenly get good shots if you are constantly brewing with different doses of coffee.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

  • WDT Tool (Distribution Tool) Helpful and cheap item, will allow you to uncluster any clustered grinded coffee once it's in your basket. This will allow for even distribution of the water running through your puck, and a good extraction.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

  • Funnel Tool Another helpful and cheap item, will allow you better funnel your dose into your portafilter and basket to make sure you don't make a mess when wrinding into your portafilter or when using your WDT tool to distribute.

    Recommended purchase: Sample item link

Total Estimated Equipment Cost: $0
